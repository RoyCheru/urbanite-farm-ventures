# Urbanite Farm Ventures

Build a premium, modern, fully responsive website for Urbanite Farm Ventures, an agricultural business located in Solai, Nakuru, Kenya.

Create a sophisticated, professional agribusiness website that builds trust and attracts both individual customers and bulk buyers. Have uploaded some few images of designs we can look into.

Homepage Sections

Navigation: Logo, Home, About Us, Products, Bulk Orders, Contact, and a prominent WhatsApp enquiry button.

Hero: A striking farm photograph, the headline “Quality Farm Products. Direct from Urbanite Farm Ventures.”, concise supporting text, and buttons for “Explore Our Products” and “Enquire on WhatsApp.”

Our Products: Four visually appealing cards for Farm Eggs, Broiler Chickens, Pigs & Live Pigs, and Other Farm Products. Each card needs a relevant image, short description, and product-specific enquiry button.

Bulk Orders: A compelling section targeting retailers, resellers, hotels, restaurants, and other business buyers, with a clear call to action.

About Us: Introduce the farm, its location, and its agricultural focus

How to Order: Three simple steps—browse products, enquire via WhatsApp, and confirm availability and pricing directly with the farm.

Contact: Display Solai, Nakuru, Kenya, a WhatsApp contact placeholder, and a clear enquiry call to action.

Footer: Logo, quick navigation, product categories, location, and contact information.

Also include a blog section. We should have icons that takes us to social media page like WhatsApp, Facebook. For WhatsApp use this number: 0727459772

Build the frontend prototype using React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS or any other recommend tech stach.Ensure excellent mobile responsiveness, accessible contrast, reusable components, smooth scrolling, working navigation, and subtle hover effects. WhatsApp buttons should open a chat with a prefilled enquiry message; 

Deliver an impressive, trustworthy, conversion-focused agribusiness homepage that encourages visitors to enquire and makes Urbanite Farm Ventures look like an established, professional business.
